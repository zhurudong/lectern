# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Concretely, in issues, pull requests and discussions here:

- criticize code, not people;
- no harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone's
  identity, background, or level of experience;
- no sexualized language or imagery, and no unwelcome personal attention;
- do not post other people's private information;
- assume the person on the other end is trying to help, and that they may be
  writing in a second language.

To report abusive behavior, use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow.
